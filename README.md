# Geospatial Analysis and Cartography Portfolio

This portfolio presents selected GIS cartography and spatial visualization projects created with ArcGIS Pro. The projects demonstrate thematic mapping, terrain visualization, proportional-symbol mapping, multi-layer spatial visualization, and professional cartographic layout design, with applications related to recreation, population, and land management in Utah.

## Selected Projects

### 1. Recreation, Lodging, and Land Management near Bryce Canyon National Park

This map presents recreation and lodging resources near Bryce Canyon National Park, including campgrounds, hotels, trails, roads, and land-administration areas. The project demonstrates multi-layer symbolization, labeling, land-management classification, and locator inset map design.

**Methods and cartographic elements:**

- Multi-layer spatial visualization
- Point, line, and polygon symbolization
- Land-administration classification
- Labeling and annotation
- Locator inset map
- Legend, scale bar, and north arrow design

[View the Bryce Canyon Recreation and Lodging Map](Bryce_canyon_recreation_lodging.pdf)

---

### 2. Ski Areas, Lift Capacity, and Recreation Trails in Salt Lake County

This map visualizes ski areas, ski lifts, and recreation trails in Salt Lake County. Trail colors represent difficulty levels, while ski-lift symbols provide information about lift capacity.

**Methods and cartographic elements:**

- Terrain and hillshade visualization
- Categorical trail symbolization
- Ski-area boundary mapping
- Multi-variable legend design
- Thematic and reference map integration

[View the Salt Lake County Ski and Recreation Map](Salt_lake_county_ski_recreation.pdf)

---

### 3. Population and Recreation Resources in Salt Lake County: Choropleth Map

This map presents the geographic distribution of population and recreation resources in Salt Lake County. Population values are represented using graduated polygon colors, while ski areas and trails show the distribution of recreation opportunities.

**Methods and cartographic elements:**

- Choropleth mapping
- Graduated-color classification
- Population data visualization
- Integration of demographic and recreation data
- Terrain basemap design

[View the Salt Lake County Population Choropleth Map](Salt_lake_county_population_choropleth.pdf)

### 4. Population Centers and Recreation Resources in Salt Lake County

This map examines the spatial relationship between population centers and recreation resources in Salt Lake County. Proportional symbols represent population size, while ski areas and trails show nearby recreation opportunities.

**Methods and cartographic elements:**

- Proportional-symbol mapping
- Population data visualization
- Integration of demographic and recreation data
- Multi-layer map composition
- Professional cartographic layout design
